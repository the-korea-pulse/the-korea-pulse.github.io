Ownership reports were the largest category in South Korea’s DART filings for week 45 of 2026. The system received 3,002 filings across 325 disclosure types. The five most common types accounted for 1,070 filings, or 35.64% of the total.

## Shareholding reports set the pace

Reports on securities held by executives and major shareholders led with 335 filings, followed by substantial shareholding reports with 265. Together, those two categories made up 600 filings, roughly one in five received that week.

Securities issuance results and provisional operating results based on consolidated financial statements each accounted for 157 filings. Prospectuses followed at 156.

## Filings spread across submitters

Shinhan Asset Management submitted the most filings, with 86. Daol Asset Management filed 42, NH Investment & Securities 38, Hanwha Asset Management 33, and IBK Asset Management 31.

Those five submitters filed 230 documents combined, or 7.66% of the weekly total. The figures show a more concentrated mix of disclosure types than of submitters: the leading five types represented over a third of filings, while the busiest five submitters represented less than a tenth.
