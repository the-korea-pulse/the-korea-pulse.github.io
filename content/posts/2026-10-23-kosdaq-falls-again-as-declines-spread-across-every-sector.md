The KOSDAQ closed lower again, and the decline widened: every sector fell in the latest session. Publishing and media reproduction and financial stocks led the retreat.

## From a mixed session to a broad decline

Construction stocks had risen in the preceding session even as the index fell. Ecopro BM was among the shares that declined. By the latest close, no KOSDAQ sector had finished higher.

## Ecopro BM’s filed figures

Ecopro BM’s regulatory filing provides company context, but does not establish why its shares fell. Revenue changed **−26.04% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)** from a year earlier, while operating profit changed **−63.24% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)**. Its operating margin was **3.12% (Q2 2026)**.
