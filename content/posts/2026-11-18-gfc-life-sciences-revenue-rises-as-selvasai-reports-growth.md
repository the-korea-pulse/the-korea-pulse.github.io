GFC Life Science’s revenue grew in the latest reported quarter, while SelvasAI reported higher sales and operating profit. The clearest measure of GFC’s performance is its regulatory filing: revenue rose **+25.79% (Q3 2025 → Q3 2026)**, and its operating margin was **11.89% (Q3 2026)**.

## GFC’s quarterly growth

The revenue increase shows that GFC sold more than in the comparable quarter a year earlier. Its operating margin shows how much operating profit it retained from that quarter’s sales. Neither figure measures the year-to-date profit increase described in the earnings report, so the quarterly margin should not be read as evidence of that reported change.

## SelvasAI’s reported gains

SelvasAI attributed its improved results to growth in medical devices and wider supply of AI solutions. Those businesses offer two potential sources of sales growth, but the material provided here does not establish how much each contributed or whether either improved profitability.
