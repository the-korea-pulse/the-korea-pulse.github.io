KS Industry, Dream Insight and UDM Tech have each announced plans to raise operating funds by issuing shares to selected investors. The placements are separate financing decisions; the share prices and resulting ownership changes will determine their effect on existing shareholders.

## KS Industry’s earlier filed performance

KS Industry’s revenue changed by −6.39% (Q3 2025 → Q3 2026), according to figures from the company’s regulatory filing. Its operating margin was 3.06% (Q3 2026). These figures describe an earlier filed period, not the terms or expected proceeds of the planned share issue.

## What the placements mean for shareholders

A placement gives the designated investors new shares. Existing shareholders’ percentage holdings can fall as a result, depending on how many shares each company issues. The final terms will therefore matter as much as the stated purpose of raising operating funds.
