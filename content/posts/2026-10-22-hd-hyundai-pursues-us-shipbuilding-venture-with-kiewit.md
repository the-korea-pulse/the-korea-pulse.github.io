HD Hyundai is pursuing a joint venture with US engineering and construction company Kiewit to build a ship block yard in the United States. The proposed yard would give the Korean group a local production base, though the venture and facility remain plans rather than completed projects.

## A foothold in US production

Ship blocks are large sections assembled before a vessel is completed. Producing them in the United States could allow HD Hyundai to carry out part of its shipbuilding work locally if the companies proceed with the venture. The reported plan concerns a block yard, not an announced full shipyard.

## The parent company’s financial backdrop

HD HYUNDAI CO., LTD.’s regulatory filing shows revenue rose +30.2% from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, while operating profit rose +262.16% over the same period. Its operating margin was 18.41% in Q2 2026. Those figures describe the parent company’s filed performance; they are not financial projections for the proposed US venture.
