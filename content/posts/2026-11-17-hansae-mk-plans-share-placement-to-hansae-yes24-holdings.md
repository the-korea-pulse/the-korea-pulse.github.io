Hansae MK plans to raise capital by issuing shares to Hansae Yes24 Holdings through a third-party placement, according to Yonhap’s account of the company’s announcement. The supplied DART record is an ownership-change notice; it does not establish the placement’s proceeds, share count or price.

## Operating figures provide context

Hansae MK’s regulatory filing shows revenue changed **+1.82% from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026**. Its operating margin was **−6.79% in Q3 2026**, indicating an operating loss for that quarter. These figures describe the filed period, not the terms or expected effect of the planned financing.

## Terms needed to assess dilution

The share price and number of new shares will determine how much existing shareholders’ stakes are diluted. Those terms are not present in the supplied ownership-change record, so the placement’s effect on ownership cannot yet be quantified from it.
