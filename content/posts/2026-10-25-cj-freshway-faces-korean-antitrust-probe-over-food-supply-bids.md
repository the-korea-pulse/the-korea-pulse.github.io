South Korea’s Fair Trade Commission has begun an on-site investigation of CJ Freshway over suspected collusion in food supply bids. The inquiry concerns an allegation; the reported investigation does not establish that the company violated competition law.

## The bids under scrutiny

CJ Freshway operates in food distribution and food services. The reports identify suspected bid collusion as the focus of the commission’s inquiry, but do not specify which tenders are under review or name other companies involved.

## Financial context

CJ Freshway’s regulatory filing shows revenue rose **+4.51%** from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, while operating profit changed **−14.23%** over the same period. Its operating margin was **2.55%** in Q2 2026. Those filed figures describe the company’s financial performance; they do not indicate whether collusion occurred.
