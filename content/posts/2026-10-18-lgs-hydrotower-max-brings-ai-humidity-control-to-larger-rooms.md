LG Electronics has launched the PuriCare HydroTower Max, a combined humidifier and air purifier aimed at larger living spaces. Its central feature is AI control that adjusts humidification according to room conditions and temperature.

## One appliance for air and moisture

The HydroTower Max combines air cleaning with water purification, sterilization and drying functions. That gives LG a broader pitch than humidity control alone: the appliance is designed to manage the air people breathe and the water it uses to add moisture. The reported features do not establish how well it performs in a particular home; that will depend on use and room conditions.

## The business backdrop

LG’s regulatory filing shows revenue rose +14.91% and operating profit rose +146.96% from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026. Its operating margin was 6.63% in Q2 2026. Those companywide figures provide context for the launch, but do not show sales or profitability for the HydroTower Max.
