Samyang Holdings and Hana Financial Group each attracted orders above the planned size of a debt offering, according to Korean press reports. Samyang sought buyers for unsecured bonds, while Hana tested demand for hybrid capital securities. The reported results show interest in both deals, though they do not establish that investors viewed the two instruments in the same way.

## Samyang’s filed results provide context

Samyang Holdings’ regulatory filing shows a year-on-year revenue change of +10.65% from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026 and an operating profit change of +56.26% over the same period. Its operating margin was 6.29% in Q2 2026. Those operating figures provide context for the company, but they do not show why investors placed bond orders.

## Demand is one step before final terms

Orders submitted during demand forecasting indicate investor interest; they are not the final amount issued. Samyang’s DART disclosures supplied here concern a subsidiary’s debt guarantee decision and its correction. They do not set out the final terms of either reported offering.
