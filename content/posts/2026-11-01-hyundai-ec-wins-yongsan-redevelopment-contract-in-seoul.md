Hyundai Engineering & Construction has been selected as the contractor for the Hangangro urban redevelopment project in Seoul’s Yongsan District. The award adds a central Seoul project to the builder’s redevelopment portfolio.

## A new project in Yongsan

The selection was made at a meeting of the project association. For Hyundai E&C, the award secures another construction role in a district where redevelopment is reshaping parts of the urban landscape.

## Financial context for the builder

Hyundai E&C’s regulatory filing shows revenue fell **−11.38%** from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, while operating profit rose **+20.66%** over the same period. Its operating margin was **3.83%** in Q2 2026. Those figures describe an earlier filed period; they do not measure the financial effect of the Hangangro award.
