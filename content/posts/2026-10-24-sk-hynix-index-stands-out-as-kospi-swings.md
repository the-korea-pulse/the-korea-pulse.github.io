The KRX SK Hynix total return index stood out during two reported Kospi rallies, leading the gains described in each closing report. It also fell in a separate session when the broader market declined. The reports show how sharply the index moved across those sessions, but do not establish what drove the moves.

## Hynix leads the advancing sessions

In one session, the Hynix index rose more strongly than the broader market as the Kospi edged higher. In another, it led gains alongside the KRX Samsung Electronics total return index while the Kospi climbed more broadly. The separate down session shows that the prominence of the Hynix index was not confined to rising markets.

## Company context, not a cause for the moves

SK Hynix, based in Icheon, reported 2025 revenue of 97.15 trillion won, according to its [company profile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Hynix). That figure provides context for the chipmaker’s scale. It does not explain the index moves, and the closing reports do not identify a company announcement or disclosure that caused them.
