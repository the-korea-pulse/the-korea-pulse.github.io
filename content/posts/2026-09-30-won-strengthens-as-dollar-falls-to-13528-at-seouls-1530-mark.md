The won strengthened against the US dollar on September 30. The dollar stood at 1,352.8 won at Seoul’s 15:30 pricing mark, down 3.9 won from the previous day’s 1,356.7 won.

## A second day of gains for the won

The move followed a larger decline in the dollar on September 29, when its 15:30 rate fell 8.4 won. Together, the two readings show the won extending its advance, though the latest daily move was smaller.

## What the rate tells us

A lower won-per-dollar quote means fewer won are needed to buy a dollar. The 15:30 figure captures the daytime market reference point; it does not, by itself, establish why traders moved the currency or where it will trade next.
