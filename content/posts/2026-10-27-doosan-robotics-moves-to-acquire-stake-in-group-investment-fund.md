Doosan Robotics has filed a decision to acquire equity in another entity. Korean press reports identify the target as a Doosan new technology investment fund and say Doosan Tesna plans to take a stake in the same fund. The filings and reports point to parallel investment decisions by two listed Doosan companies.

## The financial context for Robotics

Doosan Robotics’ latest regulatory filing shows revenue changed **+289.96%** from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, while its operating margin was **−81.57%** in Q2 2026. Those figures describe its existing business performance; they do not establish what the fund investment will earn.

The effect on Robotics’ finances will depend on the investment’s terms, when capital is contributed and how the fund’s holdings perform.
