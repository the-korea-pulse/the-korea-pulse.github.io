Samsung Electronics approached its October 8 preliminary third-quarter earnings release with brokerages expecting results above market forecasts and stronger memory growth next year. Its shares nevertheless closed down 1.29% on October 7, while SK Hynix fell 2.82%.

## Expectations before the release

The brokerage outlook points beyond the coming quarter: analysts see the prospect of faster memory growth next year. That remains a forecast. The preliminary third-quarter figures were not included in the information available for this article, so the size of any earnings beat cannot yet be established.

## The earlier filed results

Samsung Electronics’ regulatory filing shows revenue up **+130%** and operating profit up **+1,813.84%** from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026. Its operating margin was **52.18%** in Q2 2026. Those figures cover the earlier filed quarter; they are not the preliminary third-quarter results.
