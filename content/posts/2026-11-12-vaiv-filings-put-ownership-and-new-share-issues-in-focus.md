VAIV company has disclosed a contract concerning a change in management control and a shareholder record date. Korean press reports also describe planned third-party share issues, making ownership and potential dilution the immediate questions for shareholders. The available disclosure summaries do not establish how the filings and reported issues relate.

## Funding plans and ownership

The reports describe one proposed third-party allotment for purposes including operating funds and another for purposes including the purchase of securities in another company. VAIV’s filings with South Korea’s electronic disclosure system separately identify a management-control contract and a shareholder record date. The summaries provided here do not specify the investors, issue prices or terms of the control agreement, so the effect on existing shareholders cannot yet be determined.

## The financial backdrop

Figures calculated from VAIV’s latest regulatory filing show a **−28.63%** change in revenue from a year earlier **(Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)** and an operating margin of **−30.89% (Q2 2026)**. Those earlier filed figures provide context for the reported fundraising plans; they do not show how the proposed transactions would affect the company’s finances.
