Korea’s DART system recorded 4,604 filings across 313 categories in the 47th week of 2026. Quarterly reports accounted for 1,826 filings, making them by far the largest category.

## Five filing types made up most of the total

After quarterly reports came 283 reports on securities held by executives and major shareholders, 226 large shareholding reports, 173 investment prospectuses and 155 additional shelf-registration documents. Together, these five categories accounted for 2,663 filings, or 57.84% of the week’s total. The other 308 categories accounted for 1,941 filings.

## Filing volume was spread across submitters

Samsung Asset Management submitted the most filings, with 94. Korea Investment Management followed with 55, Mirae Asset Global Investments with 47, Hanwha Investment & Securities with 42, and Shinhan Asset Management with 37.

Those five submitters filed 275 documents combined, or 5.97% of the total. The week’s activity was therefore concentrated in a few filing categories, while no single submitter accounted for a large share of all filings.
