KakaoBank’s “mini My Way Savings” service has passed 300,000 cumulative accounts, the bank says. The milestone shows the appeal of a savings product that lets young users personalize how they set aside pocket money, though the account total does not show how much they have saved.

## A savings product built around personal goals

The service is designed for teenagers and lets them customize their savings experience. That approach gives young users a way to connect saving with things they want to buy, rather than treating it as an abstract financial habit.

## What the milestone measures

The 300,000 figure is a cumulative account count. It establishes how many accounts have been opened, but it does not measure current balances or how regularly customers add money. Those measures would give a clearer picture of whether the service is building lasting savings habits.
