South Korea’s DART system received 2,308 filings across 316 disclosure types in 2026’s week 41. Reports on securities held by executives and major shareholders were the largest category, with 252 filings, followed by 198 large-shareholding reports.

## Ownership reports set the pace

Together, those two ownership categories accounted for 450 filings, about one in five of the week’s total. Securities issuance results reports numbered 133, and prospectuses numbered 103. The counts show how often each form was filed; they do not measure the value of securities issued or traded.

Another 132 filings were effectiveness notices associated with a blanket registration statement for trust-type collective investment securities submitted on October 1.

## Asset managers among the busiest filers

Kyobo Asset Management submitted the most filings, at 172. MCU Tenth Securitization Specialty Company followed with 42. Samsung Asset Management filed 36, Mirae Asset Global Investments 33, and Shinhan Asset Management 31.

Kyobo’s count alone represented about 7.5% of all filings received that week. That concentration is useful context for the weekly total: a high filing count can reflect repeated submissions by one institution as well as activity spread across many companies.
