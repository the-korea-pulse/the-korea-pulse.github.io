Daou, CJ and Shinsegae have signed an agreement to acquire JoongAng Ilbo, according to Korean press reports. The deal would change the ownership of a major South Korean newspaper, but the reports’ headlines do not establish how the buyers would divide their stakes or fund the purchase.

## The ownership question

The agreement follows the group’s selection as preferred bidder, according to one report. A signed deal advances the acquisition, but the buyers’ respective roles and any conditions for completion remain unclear.

## Shinsegae’s financial backdrop

Shinsegae’s regulatory filing shows a year-on-year revenue change of +5.11% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026) and an operating profit change of +121.84% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026). Its operating margin was 9.39% (Q2 2026). Those figures describe Shinsegae’s filed performance; they do not indicate how much it would invest in JoongAng Ilbo.
