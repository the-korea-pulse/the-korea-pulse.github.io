Samsung Biologics’ public subscription drew applications well beyond the shares available, indicating strong demand for the share offering intended to help fund its planned acquisition of Polypeptide Group. The company has filed a securities issuance results report with South Korea’s financial regulator.

## Demand and the funds raised

The public subscription followed the shareholder allocation stage of the offering. The reported subscription deposits reflect money committed to applications; they are not the amount Samsung Biologics will retain from the issue. The issuance results determine how many shares investors receive and the proceeds available to the company.

## Financial context for the acquisition

Samsung Biologics’ regulatory filing shows revenue grew +18.52% from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, while operating profit grew +1.04% over the same period. Its operating margin was 42.95% in Q3 2026. Those figures describe the company’s earlier filed operating period, not the outcome of the share subscription or the planned acquisition.
