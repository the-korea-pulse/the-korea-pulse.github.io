Samyang Foods, LIG Defense & Aerospace and Shift Up shares fell in separate trading sessions following disappointing earnings reports. A stronger won weighed on Samyang’s net income, while weaker operating results drove concern about the other two companies.

## Currency pressure at Samyang Foods

Samyang Foods closed lower after reporting a decline in net income linked to won strength. Its earlier regulatory filing shows revenue rose **+39.28% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)** and operating profit rose **+46.71% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)**. The filing puts its operating margin at **22.87% (Q2 2026)**. Those figures cover the earlier filed period, not the results behind the recent share-price move.

## Earnings disappointments hit two more stocks

LIG Defense & Aerospace fell after weak results, reaching a new annual low during trading before closing sharply lower. Shift Up also declined after a steep drop in operating profit.
