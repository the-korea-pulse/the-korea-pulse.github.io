Jeil M&S and Humax have announced separate plans to issue shares to designated investors. For Jeil M&S, the financing announcement comes alongside a regulatory filing reporting a change in its largest shareholder. The available filing details do not establish whether the share issue caused that change.

## Jeil M&S’s ownership filing

Jeil M&S’s share issue names Industrial Bank of Korea among the recipients. The bank was established to expand credit for smaller businesses, but its participation alone does not explain the terms or purpose of this transaction.

Jeil M&S has also filed a notice of a change in its largest shareholder with South Korea’s financial regulator. The information provided does not identify the new shareholder or give the size of the stake.

## A separate placement by Humax

Humax announced a third-party share issue to J2B for operating funds and other purposes. It is a separate transaction; nothing in the supplied information links it to Jeil M&S’s shareholder change.
