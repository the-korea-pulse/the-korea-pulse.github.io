Krafton plans to hire at least 100 AI-focused entry-level employees in its largest new-graduate recruitment round to date. The company is framing the intake around its “AI native” strategy.

## A larger intake for AI talent

The announced scale makes AI recruitment a central part of this hiring round. The reports do not specify the roles, application timetable or how the new employees will be deployed, so the practical reach of the hiring push remains to be seen.

## Financial context

Krafton’s regulatory filing shows revenue changed **+94.88%** from a year earlier (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026), while operating profit changed **+67%** over the same period. Its operating margin was **31.85%** in Q2 2026. Those figures describe the earlier filed period; the company has not stated how much it will spend on this recruitment.
