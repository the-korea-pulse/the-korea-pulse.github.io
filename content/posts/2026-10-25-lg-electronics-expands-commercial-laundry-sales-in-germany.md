LG Electronics has secured a deal to supply commercial laundry appliances to a German laundry solutions company, giving its global business customer strategy a foothold in a market where equipment and ongoing management services can be sold together.

## A commercial customer in Germany

The agreement centers on large-capacity laundry appliances. LG is also promoting management solutions as part of its commercial laundry offering. The reported deal establishes a German customer, but the disclosed details do not give an order value, delivery schedule or number of machines.

## The broader business push

Commercial laundry sales extend LG’s appliance business beyond household buyers and into purchases by companies that operate or provide laundry services. The German agreement is one step in that expansion; its financial contribution cannot yet be assessed from the deal details reported.

LG Electronics’ regulatory filing provides company-wide context. Revenue rose +14.91% from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, while operating profit rose +146.96% over the same period. Its operating margin was 6.63% in Q2 2026. Those figures cover LG Electronics as a whole and do not measure sales from the German agreement.
