GS Engineering & Construction has been selected as the contractor for the Mokdong 12 apartment rebuilding project in Seoul, while Ssangyong Construction has won a separate rebuilding project in Busan’s Geoje 2 district. The awards give the two builders work in different cities, but their reported contract values are not established by the regulatory information cited here.

## GS E&C’s Seoul award

The Mokdong 12 project is in Yangcheon District, an established residential area of western Seoul. GS E&C’s selection identifies the builder for the project; the announcement alone does not establish when construction will begin or how the work will affect earnings.

GS E&C’s latest filed results provide context for the award. Revenue changed by −14.46% and operating profit by −35.65% from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, according to figures from the company’s regulatory filing. Its operating margin was 3.48% in Q3 2026. Those figures cover an earlier reporting period and do not include the newly announced project as a reported result.

## Ssangyong’s Busan project

Ssangyong’s award concerns the Geoje 2 rebuilding project in Busan. It is a separate contract, so the two announcements should be viewed as individual additions to each company’s construction work rather than as a joint development.

The awards show that both companies remain active in apartment rebuilding. Their effect on revenue will depend on project schedules and subsequent construction work.
