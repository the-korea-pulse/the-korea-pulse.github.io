Samsung Electronics is keeping Jeon Young-hyun at the head of its semiconductor business and Roh Tae-moon at the top of its smartphone and home appliance operations. Its latest president-level appointments also put Hwang Sang-joon and Choi Won-joon forward in memory and Galaxy, respectively.

## Continuity at the top

The appointments preserve Samsung’s two senior business leaders while changing the executives responsible for two of its best-known product lines. That gives the memory and Galaxy teams new leadership without changing who oversees the broader chip and consumer businesses.

## Financial backdrop

Samsung Electronics’ regulatory filing shows revenue rose **+126.88%** and operating profit rose **+783.12%** from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026. Its operating margin for Q3 2026 was **55.03%**. Those figures describe the filed period; they do not establish why Samsung made these appointments.
