Hyosung Heavy Industries and Germany’s Siemens plan to work together on digital power infrastructure and technology aimed at improving the efficiency of AI data centers. The collaboration gives the two companies a shared direction in a market where power supply and data center operations are closely linked.

## Power and operations in one plan

The proposed work spans both the infrastructure that delivers electricity and the systems used to run AI data centers. Its commercial value will depend on whether the companies turn that scope into products or projects customers can deploy. The reported announcement does not establish a contract value or rollout timetable.

## Hyosung’s latest filed figures

Hyosung Heavy Industries enters the effort after a stronger filed quarter. In its regulatory filing, revenue for Q2 2026 was **+10.6%** from Q2 2025, while operating profit was **+60.89%** higher. Its operating margin for Q2 2026 was **15.67%**. Those figures describe Hyosung’s earlier filed performance, not financial results from the Siemens collaboration.
