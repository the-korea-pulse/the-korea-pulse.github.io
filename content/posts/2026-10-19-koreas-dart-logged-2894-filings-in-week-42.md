South Korea’s DART disclosure system received 2,894 filings across 338 filing types in week 42 of 2026. Reports on securities holdings and documents used in securities offerings accounted for much of the activity, while no single filing type represented more than a tenth of the total.

## Ownership reports led the count

Reports on specific securities held by executives and major shareholders were the largest category, with 247 filings. Reports on substantial shareholdings followed among the five most common types, with 195. Together, those two categories made up 442 filings.

Prospectuses numbered 204, supplemental shelf registration documents 186, and material event reports 165. The five leading categories totaled 997 filings, about 34% of the week’s count.

## The busiest submitters

Woori Asset Management submitted 54 filings, the most of any filer in the digest. Kyobo Securities submitted 50, Kiwoom Investment Asset Management 43, Hanwha Investment & Securities 42, and Mirae Asset Securities 36.

These figures count documents received by DART. They do not, on their own, establish how many separate transactions took place or the value of any securities offered.
