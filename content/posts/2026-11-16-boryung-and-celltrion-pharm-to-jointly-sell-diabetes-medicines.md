Boryung and Celltrion Pharm will jointly sell the Nesina and Actos diabetes medicine families, expanding Boryung’s portfolio in the field. The arrangement brings established product lines into Boryung’s sales offering.

## A broader diabetes portfolio

The two product families are the focus of the joint sales arrangement. Its commercial effect will depend on how the companies carry out the partnership; the reported announcement does not establish a sales target or forecast.

## Celltrion Pharm’s filed figures

Celltrion Pharm’s regulatory filing shows revenue rose +17.15% and operating profit rose +48.38% from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026. Its operating margin was 14.76% in Q3 2026. Those figures describe the earlier filed period and do not measure the effect of the joint sales arrangement.
