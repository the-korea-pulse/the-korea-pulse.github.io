South Korea’s growth outlook has improved sharply: the OECD now expects the economy to expand **3.7% in 2026**, up from its **2.6%** forecast in June, while the Asian Development Bank projects **3.2%**. Both forecasts point to stronger growth this year followed by a slowdown in 2027. [OECD September outlook](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-economic-outlook-interim-report-september-2026_f751d02b-en/full-report.html) · [OECD June outlook](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-economic-outlook-volume-2026-issue-1_2d1956f0-en/full-report/korea_19213db6.html) · [ADB announcement](https://www.mofe.go.kr/nw/nes/detailNesDtaView.do?menuNo=4010100&searchBbsId1=MOSFBBS_000000000028&searchNttId1=MOSF_000000000079440)

## Exports lift the near-term forecast

The OECD’s revision adds **1.1 percentage points** to its June estimate. Its September outlook attributes the stronger forecast to industrial production and export growth, including a substantial boost in technology exports for Korea’s semiconductor industry. That gives the upgrade a clear driver, while leaving its reach across the wider economy an open question. [OECD September outlook](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-economic-outlook-interim-report-september-2026_f751d02b-en/full-report.html) · [OECD June outlook](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-economic-outlook-volume-2026-issue-1_2d1956f0-en/full-report/korea_19213db6.html)

## Growth is expected to ease next year

The ADB forecasts **2.3%** growth in 2027, and the OECD projects **2.6%**. The OECD expects more modest private consumption growth to temper Korea’s expansion. The forecasts therefore describe a strong export-led year, rather than a sustained acceleration at this year’s pace.
