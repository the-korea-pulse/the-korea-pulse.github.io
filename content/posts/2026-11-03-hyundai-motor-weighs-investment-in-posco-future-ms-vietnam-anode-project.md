Hyundai Motor is considering an investment in POSCO Future M’s synthetic graphite anode project in Vietnam. The companies are discussing priority purchasing rights for Hyundai as part of the proposed arrangement, according to Korean press reports. An investment has not been finalized.

## A potential link between funding and supply

The proposed arrangement would connect Hyundai’s access to a battery material with funding for POSCO Future M’s Vietnam project. The reported priority purchasing rights remain part of the discussions, so their scope and terms are still unclear.

## POSCO Future M’s filed results

POSCO Future M’s regulatory filing shows revenue changed **+2.8% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)** and operating profit changed **+3,351.17% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)**. Its operating margin was **3.93% (Q2 2026)**. These figures describe the company’s filed results; they do not establish the cost, financing or expected return of the proposed Vietnam investment.
