A court ruled that Korea Zinc must cancel resolutions passed at its annual shareholders’ meeting after the company restricted the voting rights of Young Poong and MBK. The decision favors Young Poong and MBK in their challenge to the meeting’s outcome.

## The dispute over the vote

The ruling puts the meeting’s resolutions in question because Young Poong and MBK were prevented from exercising voting rights. The reports do not identify which resolutions must be canceled or say whether Korea Zinc will appeal. Those details will determine the practical effect on the company’s governance.

## Business performance amid the dispute

Korea Zinc’s latest regulatory filing shows revenue rose **+66.59%** and operating profit rose **+126.79%** from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026. Its operating margin was **9.21%** in Q2 2026. Those filed figures describe the company’s performance over that period; they do not resolve the dispute over how shareholders were allowed to vote.
