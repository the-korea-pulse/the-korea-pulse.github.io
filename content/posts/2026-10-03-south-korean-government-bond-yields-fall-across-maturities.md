South Korean government bond yields fell across maturities in Seoul trading. Because bond prices move in the opposite direction to yields, the decline meant government bonds gained value.

## A market-wide move

The fall extended across the government bond market, rather than being confined to one maturity. The provided data do not establish the size of the move or a verified closing yield.

## The immediate effect

Lower yields reduce the return offered to buyers at current prices. Whether the decline persists will depend on subsequent trading and the forces shaping demand for Korean bonds.
