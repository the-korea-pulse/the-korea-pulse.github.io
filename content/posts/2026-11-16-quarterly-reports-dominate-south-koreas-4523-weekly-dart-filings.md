South Korea’s DART system received 4,523 filings across 320 types in the 46th week of 2026. Quarterly reports were the largest category, with 1,336 submissions. Together, the five most common filing types accounted for 2,278 filings, or 50.36% of the weekly total.

## Quarterly reports set the pace

Quarterly reports made up about 29.5% of all filings. Reports on executives’ and major shareholders’ holdings of specified securities followed at 288, ahead of securities issuance results reports at 257 and large shareholding reports at 239. Changes to goods and services transactions with affiliates of the same controlling party accounted for 158.

The remaining 2,245 filings were spread across the other 315 types, showing how much of the week’s activity lay outside the leading categories.

## Filing volume was spread across submitters

Mirae Asset Global Investments submitted the most filings, at 62. Korea Investment & Securities filed 34, while Hana Securities, Kyobo Securities and Samsung Asset Management each filed 32. Those five submitters accounted for 192 filings, or 4.24% of the total.

The counts describe filing frequency; they do not measure the financial size or significance of what was disclosed.
