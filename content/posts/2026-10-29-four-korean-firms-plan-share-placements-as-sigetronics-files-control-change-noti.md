Four Korean listed companies have announced plans to issue shares to selected third parties. Sigetronics is among them, and its separate regulatory filings on a control-change agreement and a shareholder record date give investors additional developments to assess. The available filings do not establish how those developments relate to its planned share issue.

## The planned placements

UTI, CI Tech, Sigetronics and Taeyoung Construction each disclosed a third-party share placement, according to reports on their announcements. The reports identify CNC Technology as CI Tech’s intended allottee, K&International as Sigetronics’ and Korea Housing Finance Corporation as Taeyoung Construction’s.

A placement can bring in capital while increasing the number of shares outstanding. Its effect on existing holders depends on terms such as the issue price and share count, which cannot be assessed from the available regulatory filings.

## Sigetronics’ separate filings

Sigetronics has filed a notice concerning an agreement related to a change of control and another concerning the closing of its shareholder register or the setting of a record date. Neither filing summary provided here establishes the terms of the planned placement or a link between the filings.

For financial context, the company’s regulatory filing shows revenue change from a year earlier of **+10.94% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)** and an operating margin of **−56.52% (Q2 2026)**. Those figures describe the filed reporting periods; they do not measure the effect of the proposed share issue.
