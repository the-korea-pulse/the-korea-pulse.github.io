South Korean companies reported sharply different third-quarter earnings trends. Operating profit fell at food makers Daesang and Ottogi but rose at Maeil Dairies and Namyang Dairy Products. Construction firms SK Ecoplant and Dongbu Construction also reported gains.

## Food makers split on operating profit

The contrasting results among food companies show why a sector-wide conclusion would be premature. Samlip separately reported growth in its overseas business through September, a sales measure that should not be read as a third-quarter operating-profit result.

## Bookook’s revenue and profit measure different trends

Bookook Securities’ regulatory filing shows a **+78.11%** change in revenue from **Q3 2025 → Q3 2026**. A separate earnings report said its third-quarter net income fell from the preceding quarter. Those measures cover different financial items and comparison periods, so the revenue gain does not establish that profit rose.
