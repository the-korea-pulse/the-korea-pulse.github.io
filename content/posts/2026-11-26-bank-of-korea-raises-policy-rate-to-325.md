The Bank of Korea raised its policy rate by 25 basis points to 3.25% on November 26, from 3.00%. The decision extends a sequence of rate increases that began in July 2026.

## How the rate reached 3.25%

The bank cut its rate twice in 2025, by 25 basis points in February and again in May, bringing it to 2.50%. It then raised the rate to 2.75% in July 2026 and 3.00% in August. November’s decision is the third increase in that sequence.

## The borrowing-rate context

The average rate on newly issued mortgage loans was 4.98% in October, according to Bank of Korea economic statistics. That figure predates the November decision, so it does not show how lenders have responded to the latest increase.
