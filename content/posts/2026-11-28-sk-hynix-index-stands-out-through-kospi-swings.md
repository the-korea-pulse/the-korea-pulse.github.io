SK Hynix’s total return index stood out on both rising and falling KOSPI closes. It was among the prominent gainers on two up days and among the decliners on a down day, making its swings a recurring feature of the market reports.

## Sector moves differed across the closes

Securities shares were among the strongest performers on the rising days. Pharmaceuticals gained on the falling day, even as the SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics total return indexes declined. The broader market’s direction did not describe every sector’s performance.

## Filed results give company context

SK hynix’s regulatory filing shows revenue growth of **+327.89% (Q3 2025 → Q3 2026)** and operating profit growth of **+632.07% (Q3 2025 → Q3 2026)**. Its operating margin was **79.66% (Q3 2026)**. Those results provide context for the company’s market prominence, but do not establish what caused the index to move on any particular day.
