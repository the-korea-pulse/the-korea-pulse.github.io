South Korean government bond yields fell in Seoul ahead of the Chuseok holiday. Lower oil prices helped support bonds during a quiet trading session.

## Oil prices and bond yields

Falling oil prices can ease concern about inflation, making bonds more attractive. The decline in crude prices was cited as a factor in the broad move lower in government bond yields.

## A quiet session before the holiday

Trading was subdued ahead of the break, so the day’s move offers limited evidence of how investors will position themselves when regular trading resumes.
