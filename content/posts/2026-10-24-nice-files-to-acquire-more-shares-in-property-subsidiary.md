NICE has filed a decision to acquire shares in NICE Property, its property and catering subsidiary. A separate Yonhap report says group company NICE Information & Telecommunication also plans to buy shares in NICE Property, pointing to investment by both companies.

## Two companies named in the transaction

NICE’s acquisition is recorded in a filing with South Korea’s Financial Supervisory Service. Yonhap separately reported the planned purchase by NICE Information & Telecommunication. The disclosed material provided here does not establish the combined cost, ownership changes or purpose of the purchases.

## NICE’s latest filed performance

NICE Holdings’ latest regulatory filing shows revenue rose **+15.63%** from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026, while operating profit rose **+2.44%** over the same period. Its operating margin was **9.33%** in Q2 2026. Those figures describe the company’s earlier filed performance, not the financial effect of the share acquisition.
