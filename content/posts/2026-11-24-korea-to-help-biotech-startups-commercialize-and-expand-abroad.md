South Korea’s Ministry of SMEs and Startups is working with Daewoong Pharmaceutical and Kyung Hee University to help promising small pharmaceutical and biotech firms commercialize their technology and enter overseas markets.

## Industry and academic partners

The initiative brings a drugmaker and a university into the ministry’s effort to support smaller firms. Commercializing a technology and selling it abroad are distinct challenges; the announcement identifies both as goals, but details of funding, eligibility and target markets were not immediately available.

Daewoong’s latest regulatory filing provides context for the company’s participation. Its revenue rose **+11.55%** and operating profit rose **+25.39%** from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026. Its operating margin was **15.53%** in Q3 2026. Those figures describe Daewoong’s business, not the scale or expected results of the new initiative.
