Third-quarter operating profit rose from a year earlier at KakaoPay, GC Green Cross WellBeing and Hyundai Green Food, according to their earnings headlines, while Netmarble reported a decline. The reports point to different outcomes among the four companies, rather than a common direction for Korean corporate earnings.

## Gains of different sizes

KakaoPay reported the strongest year-on-year increase among the companies covered here. GC Green Cross WellBeing also reported a substantial gain, while Hyundai Green Food’s increase was slight. Netmarble moved in the opposite direction.

## Netmarble’s earlier filed results

Netmarble’s regulatory filing shows revenue change of **+4.4% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)** and operating profit change of **−20.75% (Q2 2025 → Q2 2026)**. Its operating margin was **10.7% (Q2 2026)**. These figures cover the earlier filed period; they are not Netmarble’s newly reported third-quarter results.

Netmarble also filed a share-cancellation decision with the Financial Supervisory Service. The disclosure identifies a separate corporate action, but the information provided here does not establish its size or effect on earnings.
