South Korea’s Fair Trade Commission has sanctioned Celltrion over its cancellation of a diagnostic-kit manufacturing contract, finding that the subcontractor was not responsible for the circumstances behind the cancellation, according to Yonhap. Celltrion plans to challenge the decision in an administrative lawsuit.

## The contract dispute

The case turns on whether Celltrion had grounds to cancel work it had outsourced. The commission’s finding concerns the treatment of the subcontractor; Celltrion’s planned lawsuit will give the company a route to contest the sanction in court.

## Financial context

Celltrion’s regulatory filing shows revenue rose **+44.96%** and operating profit rose **+86.33%** from Q2 2025 to Q2 2026. Its operating margin was **32.42%** in Q2 2026. Those figures describe the company’s performance during the filed period; they do not establish the outcome of the contract dispute.
