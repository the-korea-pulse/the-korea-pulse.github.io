**The Korea Pulse** publishes English-language analysis of Korea's economy, financial markets, corporate activity, and public policy for a global audience of investors, analysts, and Korea-focused readers who do not read Korean.

## What we cover

We track developments across Korean markets and industry — equities, currency and monetary policy, semiconductors and biotech, real estate, and government economic policy — and explain what they mean, not just what happened.

## How our articles are made

For data stories, we calculate measures such as a weekly count of regulatory filings in DART from the underlying records. Other pieces bring together figures and reporting from several sources. Each article links to its sources.

We use AI to help draft the text. Articles can publish automatically after source-data checks, quality checks and an AI review. Flagged articles are held for further review. HW has editorial responsibility and we spot-check published work. Read our [editorial standards](../editorial-standards/).
